01 / 15

Sign in

Getting in
01

Sign in Everyone

The first screen after the intro animation. You sign up with your phone number and a one-time code, with no password to remember.

  • The language is picked from the country you are in, from your IP address: English plus that country's own language.
  • You can switch between the two at any time.
Reporting a problem
02

Map Everyone

The home tab. Every report in Prishtina is a pin, coloured by status: orange for reported, amber for in progress, blue for fixed.

  • Filter the pins by status.
  • Tap a pin to see what it is, where, and how many people see it too.
03

New report Residents

The orange camera tab. Snap a photo and the AI checks that it shows a street problem, guesses the category and severity, and writes the description for you.

  • Faces and number plates are blurred automatically before anything is saved.
  • Type the street name and pick a suggestion, or move the map so the pin in the centre is on the exact spot.
  • If you adopted the street, or neighbours did, they get a notification.
04

Report details Everyone

Everything about one problem: photo, street, status history, and a before and after slider once it is fixed.

  • Tap "I see this too" to push it up the city's Top 10.
  • Chips show the street, who adopted it, and "came back" if the same problem was fixed here in the last 90 days.
  • The person who reported it confirms the fix. "Not fixed" reopens the report.
  • Share it into the community chat in one tap.
Fixing it together
05

Fix & earn Residents

Small jobs that a neighbour can safely do, like picking up dumped bags. The AI decides which reports qualify.

  • Tap "I'll fix it" to claim a job so nobody else goes.
  • Take an "after" photo. The AI compares it with the "before" photo and gives you a badge if it is really fixed.
06

Mjeshtri Residents

Skilled volunteers (plumbers, welders, painters) who take the bigger jobs, such as a broken bench or a loose railing, especially when the city is off on Sundays.

  • Sign up by sending proof of your skill. The AI checks the document, and the city can approve it too.
  • Each mjeshtër shows their trade, area, jobs done and whether they are free on Sunday.
07

Rewards Residents

Local partners thank the people who fix things. Badges you earned are spent here.

  • For example, Zhuzuff Kitchen gives a free dinner for two for 20 badges.
  • You get a code to show at the partner.
Community
08

Community chat Everyone

One room for the whole city and one for each of the 11 neighbourhoods. Your own neighbourhood is marked, and a dot shows new messages.

  • City announcements reach every room.
09

Chat room Everyone

A normal group chat with a few city-specific parts: blue announcement banners, clean-up day cards with an "I'm coming" button, and shared reports.

  • "See translation" translates any message into your language.
  • The AI blocks insults, ads and phone numbers before they are posted.
  • Long-press to report a message. Three reports hide it.
  • The leaf button at the top opens this neighbourhood's clean-up days.
10

Clean-up days Residents

Neighbours meet to clean a street or a park. Anyone can organise one by picking the day, hour, meeting point and neighbourhood.

  • Everyone in that neighbourhood gets a notification, and a card appears in its chat.
  • People who are coming get a reminder the day before.
11

One clean-up day Residents

The details of one day: when, where (tap to open the map), how many are coming, and the organiser's notes.

  • Tap "I'm coming", or change your mind later.
  • Afterwards, the organiser ticks who came and each of them gets a badge.
  • If it is cancelled, everyone who was coming is told.
12

My streets Residents

Adopt up to five streets you care about and get a notification for every new problem on them.

  • A school or business can adopt a street under its own name, and that name shows on the street's reports.
  • Each street shows how many problems are still open.
The city
13

City Everyone

The public scoreboard for how Prishtina is doing: fix rate, fixed and in-progress counts, and the average time to fix.

  • Top 10 this month: the problems most people see, with a vote button.
  • How fast each neighbourhood gets fixed, and how many problems came back.
  • Jobs you can fix in your area, and what is happening near your home.
14

Settings Everyone

Your profile and badges, plus the way into your own reports, adopted streets and clean-up days.

  • Near-you alerts for problems around your home.
  • Language and light or dark theme.
For city crews
15

Jobs City crew

Crew accounts see today's route instead of the report button: every report the city set to "in progress", in driving order.

  • Finish a job with an "after" photo and a comment for the resident.
  • Works without signal: finished jobs wait and send when the phone is back online.

The city also has a web dashboard for officials, where they moderate the chat, review flagged reports and read a weekly AI summary. It is not part of the phone app. The screens above use example data.
